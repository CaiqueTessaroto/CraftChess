# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 5, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in **CraftChess**, a game developed by **Caique Leandro Tessaroto**.

By using CraftChess, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

CraftChess does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

However, CraftChess uses third-party services provided by Unity Technologies to provide certain game functionality, multiplayer connectivity, advertising, and related services.

Depending on how these services are used, information may be transmitted from your device to Unity or its service providers. This may include:

* Device or other identifiers;
* Advertising identifiers;
* IP address;
* Authentication or player identifiers used by Unity services;
* Approximate location information derived from technical data, where applicable to third-party advertising or service functionality;
* Device and operating-system information;
* Technical and diagnostic information related to the game or services;
* Information necessary to provide multiplayer connectivity.

CraftChess does not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, videos, or financial information.

## 2. Unity Relay and Multiplayer Services

CraftChess uses **Unity Relay** to provide multiplayer connectivity between players.

Unity Relay operates as a relay service that allows players to establish multiplayer connections without requiring the game developer to operate dedicated game servers.

According to Unity's documentation, Relay may process information such as:

* IP address;
* Authentication Player ID;
* Technical information necessary to provide the Relay service.

This information is processed by Unity to provide multiplayer connectivity and related functionality.

Unity acts as a service provider/processor for these services, while the developer is responsible for determining the applicable privacy requirements for the game.

For more information about Unity Relay's privacy practices, please refer to Unity's documentation and privacy policy:

[Unity Relay Privacy Overview](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/mps-sdk/privacy/privacy-overview-relay?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 3. Advertising

CraftChess uses **Unity Ads** to display advertisements within the game.

Unity Ads may process information such as device or other identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and certain technical information in connection with advertising services.

Depending on the applicable settings, legal requirements, and Unity's advertising services, this information may be used for purposes including:

* Delivering advertisements;
* Measuring advertising performance;
* Preventing fraud and abuse;
* Analytics and service improvement;
* Supporting the operation of advertising services.

CraftChess is configured to use **non-personalized advertising where applicable**. This does not mean that the Unity Ads service itself cannot process technical or device-related information. Third-party advertising services may process information according to their own privacy practices and applicable laws.

For more information, please review Unity's privacy policy:

[Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 4. Children's Privacy

CraftChess may be used by children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly request or directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses from children.

Where applicable, CraftChess uses advertising configurations intended to comply with applicable requirements for children, including the use of non-personalized advertising.

Because CraftChess uses third-party services, those services may independently process technical information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

Parents or legal guardians who believe that personal information has been improperly collected may contact us using the contact information provided below.

## 5. Data Sharing

CraftChess does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to and processed by third-party service providers used by CraftChess, including Unity Technologies and services such as Unity Ads and Unity Relay.

These services may process information for purposes including:

* Multiplayer connectivity;
* Game functionality;
* Advertising;
* Advertising measurement;
* Analytics;
* Security and fraud prevention;
* Technical operation and service improvement.

The information handled by these services may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, player identifiers, approximate location information, and technical information, depending on the service involved.

Third-party services may also share information with their own service providers or partners where permitted by their respective privacy policies and applicable law.

## 6. Data Security

Information transmitted by CraftChess's third-party services is generally transmitted using encryption in transit, such as HTTPS/TLS or other secure communication mechanisms provided by the respective service.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

CraftChess does not maintain a separate user account database containing users' names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information.

Data processed by third-party services may be retained according to the policies and retention practices of those services.

For information processed by Unity, users may have privacy and data-rights options available through Unity depending on the applicable service, jurisdiction, and type of information involved.

For more information about Unity's privacy practices and available privacy rights, please consult:

[Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
