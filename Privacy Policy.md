## Information Collection

The game **does not directly collect personally identifiable information**, such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

However, the game uses **third-party services** that may collect **limited device data**, such as:

- Device type
- Operating system
- Anonymous identifiers (e.g., advertising ID)
- Performance and crash data

This data is used **exclusively for the operation of the game and the display of advertisements**.
